The Invisible Threat: Air Quality & Health

Nitrogen Dioxide (NO2) is an invisible irritant that can significantly impact respiratory health. For the residents of Bristol, staying informed about daily concentrations is a vital part of urban wellness.

How to Stay Safe

Route Selection: Use the Air Data map to avoid "high-concentration" zones during peak hours. Even walking one street back from a major road can reduce your exposure by up to 30%.
