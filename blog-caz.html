Understanding the 2026 CAZ Expansion

As of April 2026, Bristol’s Clean Air Zone (CAZ) has undergone its most rigorous expansion since its inception. By broadening the boundary to include higher-density residential areas surrounding the city center, local authorities aim to tackle the "canyon effect" where NO2 lingers in narrow streets.

Early Data Indicators

Initial telemetry from our geospatial sensors indicates a 15% reduction in Nitrogen Dioxide levels during morning congestion hours (07:30 - 09:30). However, "leakage" remains a concern, as non-compliant vehicles seek alternative routes through residential outskirts.

  • Compliance Rate: Currently at 82% for private vehicles.
  • Hotspots: Bond Street remains a high-risk area despite regulations.
  • Public Transit: A 10% increase in Park & Ride usage since the expansion.
