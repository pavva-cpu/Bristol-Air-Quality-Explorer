Our Mission

Bridging the gap between urban data and public health awareness.

THE INITIATIVE

Empowering Citizens with Real Data

Bristol Air Explorer is a dedicated environmental monitoring platform designed to provide transparency into the air we breathe. We utilize raw datasets from the Bristol Open Data Portal to visualize Nitrogen Dioxide NO2 levels across the city.

Our goal is to transform complex chemical readings into actionable insights. By monitoring key sites across the city center, we provide the public and environmental researchers with the tools needed to advocate for a cleaner, healthier Bristol.

Methodology

How Data is Collected

Understanding the hardware behind the numbers.

Diffusion Tubes

Passive samplers used across Bristol to measure average NO2 concentrations over monthly periods. These provide essential baseline data for urban planning.

Real-time Sensors

Advanced electrochemical sensors provide high-frequency data, allowing us to see how traffic peaks affect air quality in real-time.

GIS Mapping

Our platform compiles these readings into geospatial heat maps, identifying "hotspots" where air quality policy intervention is most needed.

Health Safety Standards

Aligning Bristol's air quality with World Health Organization (WHO) guidelines.

Exposure Risk

WHO guidelines state that annual NO2 levels should not exceed 10 µg/m³. We identify areas currently over these thresholds.

Respiratory Care

High concentrations can trigger asthma. Our data helps at-risk groups plan safer travel routes through the city.

Daily Safety

Our mapping tools allow residents to check "Moderate" vs "High" zones before outdoor activities or school runs.
